My Computing Journey

ROBOTICS CURRICULUM LAUNCH

The launching of the Robotics course gave me my first real look at how machines follow instructions. Seeing code make something function in real life sparked my interest in how computers work behind the scenes.

LINE-FOLLOWING COMPETITION

Getting a robot to stay on a track for a competition taught me challenging real-world setups can be. As I continued to fix mistakes it helped me build patience and a habit of testing things until work.

DSTFRO '24

Taking part in the DSTFRO pushed me to improve my previous code on line-following. Now, sharing our ideas and teaming up with others helped me grow more confident explaining how things work

RSTFRO '24

Moving up to the RSTFRO showed me what high-level technologies can look like. These robots opened my eyes to various possibilities of what computers can accomplish.

DSTFRO '25

Coming back to DSTF a year later showed me how much I had grown in solving problems and working with a team. Now, we competed for maze-solving. These challenges felt more approchable than when I first started.

IOT RESEARCH

During our research, we created a smart IoT device that scans for rice infestations. The more I worked on this project, the more it felt as if Computer Science was the career that would bring meaning to what I can offer the world.

These chain of events exposed me to the coding platform. Slowly, this exposure turned into interest, and then passion, which led to pursue a degree in CS. In the future, I hope to immerse myself more into this space, and contribute my learnings and aspirations to the world through my program.

THE WEBBING EXPERIENCE

Creating my first official personal webpage a meaningful experience and a great milestone that allowed me to showcase my creativity together with what I have learned both in and out of the class. Through the process, I especially learned how HTML elements are styled through CSS and how I can apply it in gouping elements.

For every section, I almost always had a problem. One of my major challenges was designing my Hero section, which was also one of the sections I liked to make. In overcoming this challenge, I extensively learned so much about CSS styling and layout. With this project, I learned that web development requires not just code, but also intensive planning, designing, and debugging.

Furthermore, this project also made realise that I am capable of learning independently and facing challenges, especially in the midst of time pressure. Moreover, this project taught me to be patient, think clearly, resourceful, and persistent in finding solutions. From this, I learned that being a good developer is not necessarily about knowing all there is to know, but to have the constant desire to grow.
