Hello!

Hello! You can call me Jaz. I am a first-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) student at Bicol University. I am someone who enjoys discovering new things, expressing creativity, and learning about technology.

About Me

I would describe myself as a curious and creative person who enjoys exploring different interests. Music is one of my favorite ways to relax, while baking and reading allow me to spend my free time doing things I enjoy. My typical day revolves around attending classes, completing school activities, studying lessons, and finding time to unwind. I also enjoy watching videos online, especially true crime stories and scary podcasts. Although balancing academics and personal interests can sometimes be challenging, I try my best to stay motivated and make time for the things that make me happy.

My Path into Computing

My interest in computing started with my curiosity about computers, technology, and the applications I use every day. I became interested in understanding how programs work and how developers create websites and applications. This curiosity encouraged me to choose Computer Science as my college program. I know that learning programming requires patience, practice, and problem-solving, but I see these challenges as opportunities to improve. As a first-year student, I am excited to explore different programming languages and develop the skills needed to turn my ideas into actual projects.
