Reflection

I used a dark blue theme for this website because blue is my favorite color. To me, blue feels calm and steady, which matches my chill personality, while the darker shades give the page a modern, futuristic feel that fits a Computer Science student.

For the aesthetics, I wanted the page to look clean, sleek, and easy on the eyes. I used deep navy backgrounds, soft glowing gradients, rounded cards, and smooth hover effects, with light text so everything stays readable. I also kept the spacing and style consistent across every section so the whole page feels connected.

While building the website, I organized my information into clear sections such as About Me and my Schedule. The most challenging part was arranging the class schedule so that every subject appears in the right time slot, and making sure the layout still looks good on different screen sizes.

Through this activity, I learned that a good website needs planning, patience, and attention to detail. It reminded me of my time in our school publication, where small details can make a big difference. I am proud of how my page turned out, and I am excited to keep improving my skills in tech and design.
