My Path into Computing

I never imagined that I would end up in this program because Nursing was supposed to be my first choice. Computer Science was never part of my plans because I had always dreamed of becoming a doctor, and I wanted to pursue Nursing as my pre-med course. However, I didn't meet the required quota, so I tried applying to different courses, but Computer Science was the only one that offered me a slot. Until now, I still can't believe that God led me here. Maybe He has a better plan for me. I'm always thankful because even when one door closes, another one opens. I hope this program will lead me to a better future and help me achieve my dreams. Maybe in another life, I would finally get the chance to pursue Nursing.
