About Me

Bonjour! I'm Arianne Paula M. Nuylan, a first-year Computer Science student at Bicol University, College of Science. I am goal-driven, hardworking, and passionate about technology. Outside my classes, I help manage our family business and serve as an organizational officer. These responsibilities help me practice leadership, time management, and organization.

As the eldest daughter and older sister, I also carry responsibilities within our family. I am often expected to be dependable, independent, and someone my younger siblings can look up to. Balancing these expectations with my personal, academic, and organizational commitments is a part of my everyday life.

My typical day involves attending classes, completing academic tasks, fulfilling organizational responsibilities, and helping with our family business. When I have free time, I enjoy playing volleyball, practicing martial arts, cooking, listening to music, and watching anime. I also make time to explore technology and learn new things, especially when it comes to programming and web development.

I believe that learning is a continuous process. I may still be developing my skills, but I welcome challenges because they give me opportunities to improve, become more confident, and discover what I am capable of achieving.

Why Computer Science?

My interest in computing grew partly from my family. Three of my family members are already professional programmers, and seeing their experiences encouraged me to explore the field. I chose Computer Science because I want to understand how technology works, develop practical programming skills, and create things that can be useful to others. My cousin and I are still learning, so I see this program as an opportunity to build a strong foundation, learn from mistakes, and gradually become more capable in the technology industry.

A lesson from this project

Reflection

✿

Learning through creating

Creating this website helped me realize that web design is not just about writing code but also about presenting information clearly and making a website visually appealing and easy to navigate. I became more familiar with using HTML to organize content and CSS to improve its overall appearance. I also learned how details such as spacing, color combinations, layouts, and hover effects can enhance the user experience.

Although I encountered challenges, especially when adjusting the layout and styling, solving them helped me become more patient and resourceful. Overall, this project allowed me to apply what I have learned while discovering areas where I can still improve. I look forward to experimenting with new designs and strengthening my skills as I continue my Computer Science journey.
