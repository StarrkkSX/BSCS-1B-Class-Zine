Reflection

Growth takes time

While creating this website, I found myself spending quite some time reflecting on who I am, what I truly want, and the things I hope to experience in life. This activity allowed me to explore not only HTML and CSS but also my own personality, interests, and aspirations. I was able to improve my previous webpage by enhancing its elements and design, as well as changing its overall theme and color scheme. The process also came with plenty of trial and error, which taught me to be more patient and willing to learn along the way. It made me realize that growth takes time. Whether it's building a webpage or understanding myself better, both require patience, effort, and a willingness to keep trying.
