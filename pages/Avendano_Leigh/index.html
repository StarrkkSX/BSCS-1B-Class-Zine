My Typical Day

If I have somewhere to go or errands to run, I start my day by putting on makeup and getting ready. I drink my coffee or tea (unsweetened, always) sometime later in the day. When my head feels cluttered, I go for a walk, blast my music, or squeeze in a gym session. Once things slow down, I hop on a game and just play for a while. And of course, somewhere in between all of that, I’m doomscrolling.

My Journey into Computing

Life’s randomness somehow landed me in Computer Science because of a spontaneous, on-the-spot decision. Since I had no prior experience or interest, my journey has been incredibly difficult. I’ve had many setbacks and failing grades, yet I’m still learning to love the course. With time, I hope to become genuinely interested and to learn not only for grades but also to build skills for a lifetime.
