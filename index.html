About Us

38 students, one program, and a first semester full of firsts.

Meet BSCS 1B. We started the semester with different levels of knowledge about computers, programming, and pretty much everything that came with the course. Some had prior experience, while others were learning things as they went. Soon enough, we all had our fair share of coding errors, confusing lessons, group tasks, and deadlines.

Outside the usual academic routine, there’s also the everyday side of being 1B — the people, conversations, interests, and little things that make our section what it is.

That’s what 1-BITS brings together. A closer look at the students beyond their names on the class list, with a few photos, personal interests, and everything else that makes getting to know the class a little more interesting.

First semester done. More to learn, more to experience, and hopefully fewer errors next time.
